Counting in Japanese (1–10)

  • Ichi – One
  • Ni – Two
  • San – Three
  • Shi (Yon) – Four
  • Go – Five
  • Roku – Six
  • Shichi (Nana) – Seven
  • Hachi – Eight
  • Ku (Kyuu) – Nine
  • Juu – Ten

Stances (Dachi)

  • Hachinoji-dachi – Natural / Ready Stance
  • Zenkutsu-dachi – Front Stance
  • Kokutsu-dachi – Back Stance
  • Kiba-dachi – Horse Riding Stance
  • Neko-ashi-dachi – Cat Foot Stance
  • Fudo-dachi / Sochin-dachi – Immovable Stance
  • Sanchin-dachi – Hourglass Stance
  • Hangetsu-dachi – Half-Moon Stance

Punches & Strikes (Tsuki & Uchi)

  • Tsuki – Punch / Thrust
  • Oi-zuki – Lunge Punch
  • Gyaku-zuki – Reverse Punch
  • Kizami-zuki – Jab Punch
  • Nukite – Spear-Hand Thrust
  • Uraken-uchi – Back-Fist Strike
  • Empi-uchi – Elbow Strike
  • Shuto-uchi – Knife-Hand Strike

Kicks (Geri)

  • Mae-geri – Front Snap Kick
  • Mawashi-geri – Roundhouse Kick
  • Yoko-geri Kekomi – Side Thrust Kick
  • Yoko-geri Keage – Side Snap Kick
  • Ushiro-geri – Back Thrust Kick

Defenses & Blocks (Uke)

  • Age-uke – Rising Upper Block
  • Gedan-barai – Downward Sweeping Block
  • Soto-uke – Outside-to-Inside Block
  • Uchi-uke – Inside-to-Outside Block
  • Shuto-uke – Knife-Hand Block
  • Nagashi-uke – Deflecting Sweeping Block
  • Kakiwake-uke – Two-Handed Wedge Block
  • Juji-uke – Cross / X-Block

Target Levels (Dan)

  • Jodan – Upper Level (Head / Face)
  • Chudan – Middle Level (Chest / Solar Plexus)
  • Gedan – Lower Level (Groin / Legs)

Commands & Dojo Etiquette

  • Rei – Bow (Show Respect)
  • Yoi – Ready / Prepare
  • Hajime – Begin / Start
  • Yame – Stop / Return
  • Yasume – Rest / Relax
  • Mawatte – Turn Around
  • Seiza – Kneeling Posture
  • Mokuso – Meditation / Calm Focus
  • Kiai – Spirit Focus / Focusing Yell
  • Sensei – Teacher / Instructor
  • Sempai – Senior Student
  • Dojo – Training Hall (Place of the Way)
