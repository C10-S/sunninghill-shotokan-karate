Core Philosophy

The Dojo Kun

Handed down by Master Gichin Funakoshi, the five guiding principles of Shotokan Karate-Do govern our conduct inside and outside the dojo. Every principle begins with "Hitotsu" (first or one), reminding us that all five are equally paramount.

Funakoshi Tradition

The ultimate aim of karate lies not in victory or defeat, but in the perfection of the character of its participants.

01
Seek perfection of character
Jinkaku Kansei Ni Tsutomeru Koto
02
Be faithful and sincere
Makoto No Michi Wo Mamoru Koto
03
Endeavor and persevere
Doryoku No Seishin Wo Yashinau Koto
04
Respect others and maintain etiquette
Reigi Wo Omonzuru Koto
05
Refrain from violent behavior & control temper
Kekki No Yu Wo Imashimuru Koto
